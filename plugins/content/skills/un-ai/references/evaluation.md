# Final evaluation

Run this only when the entrypoint routes here: high-stakes or regulated prose, long or constraint-dense material, substantial restructuring, or an explicit quality-gate request. Ordinary edits and short audits use the compact exit gate in `SKILL.md`. Judge the whole piece, not isolated tokens. Mark a check as failed only when it materially affects the requested outcome.

## Task and truth

- Does the result perform the requested mode and respect the requested edit intensity?
- Does it preserve every required fact, supported material claim, quotation, constraint, term, stance, and real commitment?
- Did it avoid treating a sentence as supported or indispensable merely because it appeared in the draft?
- Did it avoid invented examples, numbers, sources, opinions, experiences, and certainty?
- Is every added lesson, evaluation, causal link, generalization, promise, recommendation, and next action traceable to the source or brief?
- Did it preserve whether the evidence shows sequence, correlation, attribution, or causation, including material scope and denominators?
- Are unresolved factual gaps visible when they affect use?
- Does the result fit the audience, medium, length, and format?
- Did it apply spoken, technical, promotional, or regulated-writing conventions only where relevant?

## Meaning and structure

- Can the core point be stated in one sentence after reading the result?
- Does each section or paragraph have a discernible purpose?
- Are claims supported by reasons, examples, mechanisms, or consequences where the genre requires them?
- Are transitions logical rather than decorative?
- Does the ending add a concrete consequence, decision, image, or next action instead of merely recapping?

## Clarity and specificity

- Are subjects, actions, referents, comparisons, and time frames clear?
- Did the edit protect useful names, numbers, dates, distinctions, and mechanisms?
- Did it replace unsupported importance labels with evidence or remove them?
- Do generic sentences pass the portability test?
- Are sentences easy to parse without oversimplifying the underlying idea?

## Voice

- Would the original writer recognize the vocabulary, cadence, stance, formality, and degree of polish?
- Did strong, distinctive lines survive?
- Did the edit preserve meaningful humor, bluntness, uncertainty, digression, and irregularity?
- If the source mixed registers, did the edit preserve an intentional shift or make a defensible, authorized choice rather than extrapolate from one line?
- If no voice evidence existed, did the result remain plain instead of inventing a persona?
- Did the editor avoid making every paragraph equally tidy?

## Pattern check

- Did any throat-clearing, faux-insight, hollow contrast, unsupported attribution, or importance inflation remain?
- Did any synonym cycling, weak predicate, false agency, or empty modifier reduce precision?
- Is repetition doing rhetorical or structural work, or is it mechanical?
- Are lists, headings, bold, emojis, and punctuation organizing information rather than decorating it?
- Does the ending avoid a generic forecast, moral, invitation, or mic-drop line?
- If a broad pledge remained, does it carry a distinct commitment rather than ceremonial optimism?
- Did the revision avoid introducing new slogan symmetry, neat three-part rhythm, hollow contrast, or engagement bait?

Do not pursue zero pattern matches. A construction passes when it is accurate, useful, intentional, and suited to the format.

## Korean-specific checks

Apply these only to Korean passages.

- Did the result preserve the original speech level, honorific relationship, and any intentional change of speaker or register?
- Did it preserve whether each claim is a fact, possibility, forecast, obligation, permission, prohibition, wish, or promise?
- Are omitted subjects still unambiguous, and were subjects restored only where responsibility or reference required them?
- Did the edit avoid adding English-style pronouns or repeating the subject in every sentence?
- Are particles, modifiers, and long adnominal clauses close enough to their targets to prevent a second reading?
- Did it reduce double passives and vague nominal predicates without forcing an actor into natural state descriptions or UI copy?
- Are established technical terms, product names, identifiers, legal phrases, quotations, numbers, dates, and units unchanged where required?
- Did it avoid replacing one stable term with several synonyms merely to vary the wording?
- Are sentence endings and lengths shaped by the relationships among ideas rather than by a quota for variety?
- Are connective adverbs, contrast formulas, lists, headings, bold, punctuation, and emojis serving logic, retrieval, genre, or voice rather than decoration?
- If the text is spoken, does it sound natural aloud without erasing contractions, questions, repetition, or the speaker's breathing points?
- Did any attempt to remove a Korean pattern introduce a new claim, stronger certainty, artificial slang, literary flourish, or unfamiliar translation of a standard term?

If a Korean-specific check fails, use [korean-patterns.md](korean-patterns.md) to select only the category files implicated by the failed passage. Do not load all A–J categories by default. Consult [korean-examples.md](korean-examples.md) when a preserve-versus-revise decision remains ambiguous. Do not report pattern counts as an AI-authorship probability.

## Mode-specific checks

### Draft

- Does the draft use only authorized facts and stance?
- Is any placeholder or assumption unmistakable?

### Edit or rewrite

- Is the degree of intervention proportional to the problem and request?
- Did the revision solve causes rather than swap flagged words for synonyms?
- Are change notes included only when requested or useful?

### Audit

- Does each finding quote enough exact text to verify it?
- Does each diagnosis explain the effect in this context rather than cite a blacklist?
- Are repeated instances grouped without hiding important examples?
- Did the audit avoid rewriting, scoring, or inferring AI authorship unless requested?

## Stop rule

Fix failed checks, then review the affected passage once more. Stop when remaining edits are subjective preferences or would weaken factual precision, purpose, or voice.
