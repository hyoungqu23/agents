# Exploration and reproduction

Keep a small evolving log: observation → possible cause/behavior hypothesis → next
check that distinguishes it → result/remaining uncertainty. New leads may extend
scope within the charter, but cannot remove initial required checks or extend the
run indefinitely. Park unresolved leads with evidence and reentry conditions.

Try a relevant contrast: individual endpoints versus range selection, another valid
role when already authorized, same input after reload, empty/error versus successful
empty data, or a normal unaffected path. Do not manufacture a defect via internal
state injection. For temporal problems choose meaningful pre/post or short video
where the tool preserves the intended session. Capture only what supports the claim.

Classify actual evidence as reproduced (same conditions confirmed again), observed_once,
intermittent, unverified or needs_intent. Record attempts and differing conditions.
A failed repeat does not erase the original observation or prove no defect. Do not
invent repeated reproduction, issue minimums, quantitative scores or root causes.
Read-only reviews of the same stored image from functional, CJK, visual and cross-page
perspectives are optional; agreement does not prove accuracy.
