# Problem document starting structure

Use with artifact-contract.md. This is a starting outline, not an obligation to fill
all sections or placeholder text. Adapt it to the user's actual purpose and existing
format. A small feature should remain a small document.

1. **User and situation** — whom this affects and when. Unprovided persona details
   stay unknown or explicitly hypothetical.
2. **Current behavior and workaround** — supported current steps, constraints and
   what users do today. Do not assume “no existing solution” when it was not checked.
3. **Problem statement** — bounded friction, its supported effect, and why the user
   wants to explore it. Distinguish the proposal from the need.
4. **Evidence, assumptions and decisions** — EV/AS/DEC IDs with source locations.
   Exact facts and qualifications survive rewriting.
5. **Desired outcome and boundaries** — user result, constraints, excluded work.
   Do not specify screens, technology or invented numeric success targets.
6. **Open decisions and next evidence** — OD IDs, effects, blocking/defer status,
   revisit conditions, and a concrete next observation if evidence is missing.
7. **Update history** — only when updating: what changed, why and which IDs remain.

Explain absent information rather than padding sections. End with document maturity
and limitations, not a declaration that research or implementation is complete.
