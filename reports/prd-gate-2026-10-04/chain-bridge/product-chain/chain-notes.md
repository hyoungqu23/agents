# Synthetic personal-workflow input
This is a synthetic evaluation request, not measured customer research.
I keep existing reading notes with existing exact tags but repeatedly open unrelated
notes when trying to find one by a chosen tag. My current workaround is to scan the
note list. I want a personal tool that returns exactly the existing notes carrying
my selected existing tag and lets me open their stored content. Notes/tags already
exist; no tag editing, team sharing, new authentication or quantitative speed target.
An empty lookup must be visibly distinct from a failed lookup. Do not decide layout,
architecture or storage. I want to design select/open/empty/failure user flows next.
No blocking product policy choice remains for that narrow flow scope; whether the
existing data can be read is an integration assumption to check later, not a new
product-policy decision. Do not claim market validation or performed measurements.
